Deal, Sales Account, Pipeline, Pipeline Stage, Deal Type, Deal Status Type and Deal Role now match a name by any part of it rather than only from the start, so a filter box finds a record from a word in the middle of its name. Deals are also searchable by account name and deal number, which were not searchable at all before, so a deal can be found by the customer it belongs to or by the number printed on it.
