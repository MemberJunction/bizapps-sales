A pipeline can route its close-won tasks to a role: the new `Pipeline.CloseWonAssigneeRoleID` (Close Won Assignee Role) assigns each Order Review and Contract Processing task to that MJ role, so every holder of the role sees it. When a pipeline has both a Close Won Assignee person and a role, the person gets the task.
