Logging an activity from a deal now lets the rep choose who it is linked to: the deal's account and primary contact start ticked and can be unticked, the billing contact and buying team can be ticked, and any other person or organization can be searched for and added. The Activity panel shows who the selected activity is linked to. `Log Activity` gains an optional `Parties` input (metadata); without it the action behaves as before. (golive #408)
