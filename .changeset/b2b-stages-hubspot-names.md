The default B2B pipeline uses the sales team's HubSpot stages: Discover 10%, Develop 20%, Validate 40%, Propose 60%, Agreement 90%, Closed Won 100%, Closed Lost 0%. Validate is new. Companies that already have a copy of the pipeline keep their stages until their data is updated.

Lead source types use the same HubSpot list: Inbound: Website/Phone, Inbound: Social Media, Inbound: Partner, Inbound: Webinar, Outbound: Campaign, Outbound: Individual Prospecting, Referral: Internal, Referral: Partner, Referral: Client, Upsell: Existing Client and Event Marketing. The six original types are renamed in place. The Internal loss reason becomes Other.
