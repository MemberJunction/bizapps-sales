A deal closed through `Sales.CloseDeal` now records `ActualCloseDate` as the business day it closed on, read from `BusinessTimeZoneEngine` for the deal's company, instead of the UTC day (bc-aidp-next-golive#168). Before, a deal closed in the evening Central (from 6 PM, 7 PM in daylight time) was stamped with the next day, so a win on a month's last evening counted in the next month's bookings and win rate. Existing rows are not rewritten. The close-won tasks' `DueAt` now counts `DueInDays` from that business close day (`ActualCloseDate`) rather than from the UTC day of `ClosedAt`. It is still stored as UTC midnight of the due day, the value the tasks app's own date picker stores for a hand-made task due that day, so after a close at 9:30 PM Central on 30 September a five-day task now holds 5 October (`2026-10-05T00:00Z`) where it held 6 October. The tasks app shows and ages `DueAt` in the viewer's zone, so in Central it lists that task as due Oct 4 and overdue from 7 PM that evening, exactly as it does a hand-made task due 5 October; for an evening close that is one day earlier than before, and for a daytime close nothing changes. The contracts seam input also carries `ExecutionDate` and `StartDate` as `YYYY-MM-DD` rather than the server's local rendering of the date.
