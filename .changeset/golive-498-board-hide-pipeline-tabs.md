With the Hide pipelines setting on, the Board no longer shows its row of tabs or the Company label above the columns (golive #498). Each tab was named by its pipeline's company, so a company with two pipelines appeared twice. The Board now shows every pipeline the Selling company and Product company filters allow, one lane each. A lane's heading adds its pipeline's name only when two lanes in view belong to the same company. The Dashboard's Open deals by stage tabs follow the same rule. The Sales header, the Dashboard, Board and Settings menu items, and the All deals, Accounts and Contacts headings no longer carry a line of explanatory text. Several dashboard labels and tooltips are shorter: the Needs attention and Expected to close headings, the stalled-in-stage row and board flag (formerly idle), the No owner and product company tooltips, and the Hide pipelines description.
