The Deals dashboard gets a filter bar and a period that bounds every figure. Period offers This quarter, Last quarter, Next quarter, This year, Last year, All time and a custom range; Selling company, Product company, Pipeline and Deal owner (with My deals) narrow every figure. Won counts wins closed in the period; Commit, Open pipeline, Weighted pipeline, the forecast, Open deals by stage, Commit by deal owner and the dated Expected to close buckets count open deals expected to close in it, and the forecast card says how many open deals have no close date. The page has five clickable tiles (Won, Commit, Open pipeline, Weighted pipeline, Win rate), a Needs attention list (past close date, no owner, no close date, idle past stage limit), and a Deals list showing the selected figure as a chip with Clear. Every number lists its deals with one selected style. Period, filters and custom dates are kept in the URL. `Sales: Dashboard Summary`, `Sales: Win Rate by Count and Value` and `Sales: Deal Roster` take the four filters as lists, the summary returns the forecast segments, weighted pipeline and attention counts, and the roster returns each deal's product companies; applied with `mj sync push`.
